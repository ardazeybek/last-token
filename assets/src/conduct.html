Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome here.

Whatever your experience, background or identity. Three simple promises keep it that way.

Be kind and patient

Especially with newcomers. Every expert was once asking the "obvious" question.

Ideas, not people

Argue about the code as hard as you like. No insults, no harassment, no personal attacks.

Assume good intent

Ask before you judge. Most rough messages are just written in a hurry.

Something went wrong? Tell the maintainer by email. Comments, issues or pull requests that break these promises may be edited or removed; repeat offenders may be blocked.
